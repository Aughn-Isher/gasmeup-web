GasMeUp vs GasBuddy

The honest comparison, from the small paid app's side of it.

GasBuddy is the gas app everyone knows: free, huge community, prices reported by drivers. GasMeUp is a small paid app with one job. They answer different questions, so here's the difference laid out plainly, and when each one is the right pick.

Why pay when GasBuddy is free?

Because free has a business model too. Free gas apps earn their money from ads and sponsored placements, which crowds the screen and puts another customer between you and the answer. GasMeUp's only customer is you. Live scans cost us real money, about 4 cents each, and a small price is what keeps the answer honest: no sponsored stations, no selling your data, no rewards program steering you anywhere.

The other difference is the math. A station three miles out of your way isn't a deal, no matter what the sign says. GasMeUp counts the drive into every price and hands you the winner, instead of a map you have to work out yourself.

When GasBuddy is the better pick

If you want a free app, a big driver community, price maps to browse, and rewards programs, GasBuddy does all of that and GasMeUp doesn't try to. If you want one answer that already counted the detour, that's what GasMeUp is for. The annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial, so you can check the math against your own streets before paying anything.
