Questions

Good to know

How is the "best pick" chosen?

We count what the fill-up costs you in total, including the drive to get there. That's why a close station can beat a cheaper one across town. You always see the full list, and the crown is just our math.

Do I need an account?

No. GasMeUp signs you in anonymously on first launch. There are no credentials and no email to type.

Where do the prices come from?

Live third-party price data, scanned at the moment you tap. Prices move fast, so always confirm at the pump. GasMeUp points you to the best-value stop; it can't promise the number on the sign.

What's Highway Mode?

On a long drive, GasMeUp scans the road ahead instead of just the area around you, and shows the best stop before you reach it. You pick the right exit instead of the first one.

Why is it paid?

GasMeUp is a paid app: $1.99 a month, or $17.99 a year with a 7-day free trial. No ads, no selling your data, no rewards program. Live scans cost us real money, about 4 cents each, and a small price is what keeps the answer honest.
